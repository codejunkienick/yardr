The train waits for a person: Christopher decides. No agent works this stage.
The train carries the question and the options, written by whoever sent it
here; if it does not, ask them before deciding.

The train leaves once the answer is on it, as a note. The stage has no
outcomes; the person answering moves it (`yardr review -g decisions`, or
`yardr bead advance <train> --to <stage>`):

- to `open`, when the train can go on: with no child open it is integrated
  again, and an answer that needs work is a new child, added first;
- to `backlog`, when the answer changes what the train is and the yardmaster must
  reshape it.

When a train arrives the yard puts a card for the decisions on it, offering
these moves, and picking one moves the train: the person's words with the pick
stay on it. A train with an ask already open gets no card, unless that ask is
the only one and a question of options: where it names a move for each option
(`yardr bead ask --move`) and the stage offers each, it becomes the card, its
options the choices and each firing its move; where it names none, or a move
no longer offered, it is quoted on the stage's card of moves instead. Either
way it is taken back, so it is answered once, on the card. A card gives way to
an ask put after it, and any other ask is what the person answers: the train
is moved by hand once it is.

## Ask
kind: moves
question: Where should this train go next?
context:
- The train waits for a person; what it needs is in its notes.
- Pick where it goes. Your words with the pick stay on the train for whoever works it next.
